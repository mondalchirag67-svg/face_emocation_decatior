Engineering Workflow

How FaceSense AI Works

From photon capture on your webcam sensor to high-confidence probabilistic softmax classification, every step is optimized for ultra-low latency.

01
Face Localization

OpenCV Haar Cascades scan frames to extract face coordinates (x, y, w, h). Handles multi-face detection, varying angles, and dynamic lighting conditions.

02
Crop & Alignment

The Region of Interest (ROI) is cropped with 8% physiological padding, converted to single-channel grayscale or RGB, and resized to target dimensions (48x48 or 224x224).

03
Deep CNN Inference

Normalized tensors X / 255.0 pass through convolutional blocks, BatchNormalization, ReLU activations, and Dropout layers to isolate spatial micro-features.

04
Softmax Analysis

The final dense layer emits 7 logits scaled via Softmax into percentage probabilities. The dominant emotion and bounding boxes are rendered directly onto the HTML5 canvas HUD.
