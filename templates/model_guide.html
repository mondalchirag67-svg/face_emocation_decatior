How CNNs Learn Emotional Action Units

Unlike traditional computer vision that relied on hand-crafted Gabor filters or SIFT descriptors, Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) automatically learn hierarchical spatial representations directly from raw pixel matrices:

  • Early Layers (Layers 1-2): Filter kernels learn localized primitives like edge orientations, skin luminance gradients, and contrast boundaries.
  • Mid-Level Layers (Layers 3-4): Assemble edges into anatomical motifs—eyebrow curvatures, nasolabial folds, pupil apertures, and lip corners.
  • Deep Layers & Dense Head: Combine motifs into global relational patterns that correspond to FACS Action Units, producing a 7-dimensional probability vector via Softmax.

The Mini-VGG Architecture for Emotion Detection

Optimized specifically for the FER-2013 dataset: compact footprint (~1.3M parameters), rapid CPU inference (<25ms), and high resistance to overfitting.

Block 1 Shape: 48 × 48
Low-Level Feature Block
  • Conv2D (32 filters, 3×3)
  • BatchNormalization
  • Conv2D (32 filters, 3×3)
  • MaxPooling2D (2×2 pool)
  • Dropout (0.25)
Block 2 Shape: 24 × 24
Mid-Level Motifs Block
  • Conv2D (64 filters, 3×3)
  • BatchNormalization
  • Conv2D (64 filters, 3×3)
  • MaxPooling2D (2×2 pool)
  • Dropout (0.25)
Block 3 Shape: 12 × 12
High-Level Action Units
  • Conv2D (128 filters, 3×3)
  • BatchNormalization
  • MaxPooling2D (2×2 pool)
  • Dropout (0.25)
Dense Head Output: 7 Probs
Classification Head
  • Flatten (4,608 vector)
  • Dense (256 units + ReLU)
  • BatchNormalization
  • Dropout (0.50 rate)
  • Dense(7, Softmax)

Hyperparameter Tuning & Optimization Cheatsheet

Parameter Recommended Value Rationale & Impact on Emotion Detection
Batch Size 64 Provides balanced gradient estimates while fitting comfortably into 4GB+ GPU VRAM.
Initial Learning Rate 1e-3 (0.001) with Adam Swift convergence on early conv filters; decaying by 50% on plateaus prevents oscillation.
Dropout Rates 0.25 (Conv) • 0.50 (Dense) Crucial for FER-2013 to stop the network from memorizing individual face identities.
Class Weights Inverse frequency weighting Disgust only accounts for ~1.5% of FER-2013 samples. Weighting prevents disgust starvation.
Face Alignment Padding 8% - 10% border expansion Ensures chin and forehead landmarks are not clipped by aggressive face detectors.
